Chowmahalla Palace

The Royal Residence of the Nizams

Chowmahalla Palace is one of the most beautiful historical landmarks in Hyderabad. It was the official residence of the Nizams of Hyderabad.

The palace is famous for its grand courtyards, elegant halls, beautiful chandeliers, and a unique combination of architectural styles.

The palace complex reflects the royal lifestyle, traditions, and cultural richness of the Nizams. Today, it attracts visitors who want to explore Hyderabad's royal history.
