Charminar

The Iconic Symbol of Hyderabad

Charminar is one of the most famous historical monuments in Hyderabad. It was built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, the founder of Hyderabad.

The monument is famous for its four grand arches, four minarets, and beautiful architectural design. It stands in the heart of Old City Hyderabad.

The area around Charminar is known for traditional markets, colourful bangles, pearls, and delicious Hyderabadi food. Today, Charminar remains an important symbol of the city's history and cultural heritage.
