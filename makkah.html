Makkah Masjid

A Monument of Faith and History

Makkah Masjid is one of the largest and oldest historic mosques in India. It is located near Charminar in the heart of Old City Hyderabad.

Its construction began during the rule of Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah in the 17th century and was completed during the reign of Mughal emperor Aurangzeb.

The mosque is known for its impressive prayer hall, large arches, stone architecture, and peaceful surroundings. It is an important part of Hyderabad's religious and historical heritage.
