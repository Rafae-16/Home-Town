Frequently Asked Questions

What are the famous places in Old Hyderabad?

Charminar, Makkah Masjid, Chowmahalla Palace and Laad Bazaar are popular attractions.

What food is Hyderabad famous for?

Hyderabad is famous for biryani, Irani chai and Osmania biscuits.

Is Old Hyderabad suitable for photography?

Yes. Its historic monuments, architecture and traditional markets offer many photography opportunities.

How can I learn about each monument?

Visit the Famous Places section of this website and select a monument to explore its history.
