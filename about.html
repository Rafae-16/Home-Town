History of Old City Hyderabad

A Journey Through Time

Old City Hyderabad is one of the most historic and culturally rich areas of Hyderabad, Telangana. The city was founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, the fifth ruler of the Qutb Shahi dynasty.

The most famous landmark is Charminar, built in 1591. It is known for its four grand arches and beautiful architecture. Charminar has become the symbol of Hyderabad.

Makkah Masjid is another important historical monument. Its construction began during the Qutb Shahi period and was completed during the reign of Mughal emperor Aurangzeb.

During the rule of the Nizams, Hyderabad developed into an important centre of culture, trade, and architecture. Chowmahalla Palace reflects the royal history of this period.

Old City is also famous for Laad Bazaar, traditional bangles, pearls, Hyderabadi biryani, Irani chai, and Osmania biscuits. These traditions continue to attract visitors from around the world.

Today, Old City Hyderabad beautifully preserves its historic monuments, traditional markets, rich culture, and royal heritage. It remains one of the most popular places to explore in Hyderabad.
